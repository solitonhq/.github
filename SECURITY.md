# Security policy

This is the default security policy for repositories in the Soliton organisation that do not have their own.
A repository's own `SECURITY.md` always takes precedence.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the affected repository: **Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**.
Include the affected version, the impact (what could an attacker do?), steps or a proof of concept, and any suggested fix.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within **5 working days** and a first assessment within **10 working days**.
- A fix or mitigation for confirmed high-severity issues within **30 days** where technically possible.
- Coordinated disclosure: we publish an advisory together with the fixed release and ask for at most **90 days** before
  public disclosure.
- Credit in the advisory unless you prefer to stay anonymous.
- Research that follows this policy, without accessing other people's data, disrupting services or disclosing before a fix,
  will not be met with legal action.

More detail: <https://solitonhq.github.io/security/>
