# Getting help

- **Something is broken:** open a bug report on the relevant repository. Include the version and the error text, and
  remove secrets and private hostnames first.
- **An idea or a question:** use the repository's Discussions, or open a feature request.
- **A security problem:** do not open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Soliton is a community project without a support contract or response-time guarantees. A well-described, reproducible
report is the fastest way to a fix.

Other ways to reach us: <https://solitonhq.github.io/contact/>
